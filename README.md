# Nano Riot

Build water cannons. Earn points. Protect Liberty Square.

Play at **https://maomaxgame.github.io/Nano-Riot/** once GitHub Pages is enabled.

Move five blue police squads and build non-lethal defenses to stop red hooligans.
Start with 100 points. A water cannon costs 50, a barricade 40, and spike strips 30.
Each hooligan arrested earns 3 points; a boss earns 15. Water cannons spray water
automatically; barricades block hooligans and spike strips reduce their resolve.

Clear 20 waves to finish a district, then continue endlessly if you wish. Choose
between Liberty Square and Canal District in the menu. Each ordinary breach removes
4 base health; a boss reaching the square ends the district. Lose at zero base health.

## Controls

- Build: choose a water cannon, barricade or spike strips, then click/tap a street.
- Construction stays selected until canceled. T toggles water-cannon building;
  arrows move the preview and Enter places it. T/Escape/right-click cancels.
- Hold and drag a barricade or spike strips to orient them; Q/E rotates the preview.
  Use [ / ] to cycle defenses; select one to upgrade, repair or demolish it.
- Select police: click/tap numbered markers, press 1–5, or drag a box around them.
- Move: click/tap a street or hold WASD. Drag from a clear street to move and face
  the group in a line. Hold Ctrl/Command to face the mouse.
- Camera: right-drag or arrow keys; scroll or +/− to zoom; Reset view to recenter.
- Right-click deselects or closes a menu. Space pauses; R restarts; Escape opens the menu.
- The menu offers a tutorial, English/French, sound and reduced-motion preferences.

Fictional, non-graphic, non-lethal strategy simulation.
